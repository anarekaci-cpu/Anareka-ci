Become a member of ANAREKA-CI

Founded in 2025, ANAREKA-CI continues the work of SYNAREK-CI, a union active since 1996, serving the restaurateurs, kiosks and attiéké producers of Côte d'Ivoire.

Who can join?

Fixed attiéké kiosks Traditional restaurants Street vendors Attiéké producers Maquis & street eateries

Why join us?

  • Official recognition

    Get the ANAREKA-CI label and gain credibility with your customers.

  • Free training

    Access training in hygiene, management and food processing.

  • Professional network

    Join a national network of attiéké restaurateurs and kiosks.

  • Access to funding

    Get support in obtaining grants and microcredit.

  • Visibility in the directory

    Your kiosk will be listed in our official online directory, currently being rolled out.

  • Defence of your rights

    The association represents you before the authorities and institutions.
